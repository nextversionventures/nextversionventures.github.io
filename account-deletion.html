Delete your Go The Distance account or data

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your account from within the Go The Distance app.

  1. Open Profile & stats from the app's home screen.
  2. Scroll to Account.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation, then choose Delete account again to finish.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. It removes your account, sign-in details, active sessions, email-verification information, connected sign-in methods, and synced fitness activity.

Shared race history is retained so completed and in-progress races remain understandable to the people you played with. It may continue to show the display name and progress you shared in those races, but it is no longer connected to an account that can sign in.

Delete your data and keep your account

To delete private app data but continue using your account:

  1. Open Profile & stats from the app's home screen.
  2. Scroll to Account.
  3. Choose Delete my data.
  4. Read the confirmation, then choose Delete my data again to finish.

This permanently removes synced fitness activity, activity assignments, saved feed-reading state, account-linked diagnostic identifiers, and private on-device settings. Your account, sign-in details, and active sessions remain available. Shared race history is retained so other players' races continue to work.
