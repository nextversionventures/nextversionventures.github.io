Go The Distance Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Go The Distance is a social fitness game published by Next Version Ventures ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how the Go The Distance mobile app handles information when you use it.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide to create and use an account:

  • your display name;
  • your email address and password, if you choose to create an account with them; and
  • game information such as your player ID, round membership, map progress, activity entries, game actions, and messages or entries shown in a round feed.

Passwords are stored as password hashes, not as readable passwords.

If you choose to connect Google Health Connect on Android, the app asks for permission to read steps, exercise sessions, distance, and elevation gained. It reads a rolling recent period and sends daily summaries used by the game, including the activity category, date and time range, distance, elevation, duration, and step total. Google Health Connect data is used only to provide the app's fitness-game features; it is not used for advertising or sold.

The app stores sign-in tokens, selected settings, notification preferences, and limited diagnostic checkpoints on your device so that it can keep you signed in, remember your choices, and help diagnose problems.

Go The Distance does not collect, use, store, or share your precise or approximate location information.

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • create and secure your account and let you sign in;
  • operate the game, calculate progress, prevent duplicate activity entries, and show your progress to the people in your round;
  • send optional on-device notifications about activity that is about to expire;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app; and
  • comply with applicable law and protect the security of the app and its users.

How information is shared

Your display name, game progress, activity-derived game results, and round-feed entries are visible to other players in the same game round. Your email address, password, authentication token, and raw Health Connect records are not shown to other players.

We use service providers that process information for us to run the app. This includes our hosting and database providers, and Sentry, which receives crash reports and limited technical information such as device and operating-system details, app version/build information, an internal player ID, the app screen where an error occurred, error messages, and stack traces. The app does not intentionally include health data, passwords, authentication tokens, email addresses, player names, session replay, or performance tracing in Sentry reports.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to enforce our rights, or to protect users, the app, or the public. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Your choices

Health Connect is optional. You can decline or revoke its permissions in Android settings or Health Connect. You can instead enter activity manually. You can enable or disable activity-expiration notifications in the app or in your device settings.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account. We may retain information where necessary for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or legitimate recordkeeping.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We retain information for as long as needed to operate the app and for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Children's privacy

Go The Distance is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
