How it works

1. Start a room

Open GameRoom on the shared host screen and create a room for your group.

2. Join by phone

Friends scan or enter the room code on their phones. No extra controller required.

3. Pick a game

Choose an original minigame and let the room decide who has the sharpest ideas, fastest guesses, or strangest pitch.

Games for the group

Think together

Find the surprising answer you share in Great Minds, solve hidden-order puzzles in Fit 3, or plot a word’s place in Coordinates.

Make something weird

Build a company from scratch in Pitchroom, fill in wild prompts in Blanked Out, or trade strokes in Inkdrop.

Bring the whole room

GameRoom is designed for a shared screen and a group of phones—perfect for a living room, game night, or a casual hangout.
