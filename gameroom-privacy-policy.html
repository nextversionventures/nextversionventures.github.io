GameRoom Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

GameRoom is a party game service published by Next Version Ventures ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how GameRoom handles information when you host or join a game.

Information used to play

GameRoom uses the room code, player name, game choices, answers, drawings, guesses, votes, scores, and other game actions you provide to run a game. This information is shared with the people participating in the same room as needed to play and display the game.

GameRoom stores limited session information on your device, such as a room or player session, settings, and audio preferences, so that the game can stay connected and remember your choices.

How we use information

We use game information to operate GameRoom, connect players to a host room, display the shared game, troubleshoot service issues, and protect the security and reliability of the service.

How information is shared

Your player name and game activity are visible to other people in your room. We may use service providers that process limited technical information to host and operate GameRoom. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Your choices

You can choose the name you use in a room and can leave a room at any time. You can clear GameRoom’s locally stored data through your browser or device settings.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We retain information only as long as needed to operate, secure, and improve the service, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Children's privacy

GameRoom is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email nextversionventures@gmail.com.
